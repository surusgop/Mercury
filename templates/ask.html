    Using Mercury

    Mercury answers plain-English questions about the voter file. Type a question and press Enter. It looks up the data on Databricks and writes the answer.

    Asking questions about the voter file

    • What's covered: the Illinois, Wisconsin and Michigan voter files, ballot history back to 2012, and Illinois precinct results for the 2020, 2022 and 2024 general elections.
    • Everyday words work: Republican → Conservative, Democrat → Liberal, Unreliable voter → Turnout voter, Base Republican → Base Conservative.
    • Follow-ups work: the chat remembers what came before, so "now break that down by county" works.
    • Name the state. Answers come back faster and more accurately.
    • It's built for totals and breakdowns, not for looking up one person.

    Example questions

    On a new chat, the grey buttons under the heading are ready-made questions. Click one to put it in the box, edit it if you like, then press Enter. More to try:

    • How many voters in Wisconsin voted by mail in the 2022 general election?
    • Who won the 2024 governor race in Cook County, precinct by precinct?
    • Show me the top 10 zip codes in Illinois by number of registered voters.

    Getting a spreadsheet

    Ask for it: "give me that as a CSV." The file appears under Files from this chat above the message box. Click it to download, then open it in Excel or Google Sheets.

    Your chats

    • Your chats are private: only you can see them. Every chat is saved and listed under Recents.
    • Click a chat to reopen it, then keep asking. Select it to Rename or Delete it.
    • Long questions keep running if you switch tabs, close the tab or reload. Come back to the chat to see the answer.

    Troubleshooting

    • Slow? Most questions take 10–30 seconds. The first question of the day can take a few minutes while the Databricks cluster starts.
    • Something went wrong? Your chat is saved, so ask again or reword it.
    • Answer looks off? Ask "can you double-check that?" or "show me the raw numbers."
    • Still stuck? Contact the Surus tech team.
